# FoodBridge – Rescuing Surplus Food, Feeding Communities

FoodBridge is a web-based food donation platform designed to reduce food wastage by connecting people and organizations with surplus food to communities and individuals who need it.

The project provides a simple and user-friendly interface where users can explore available food donations, understand the donation process, and contribute towards reducing food waste.

---

## Problem Statement

A large amount of edible food is wasted every day while many people struggle to access sufficient food.

Food donors and people in need may not have a simple platform to connect with each other. FoodBridge aims to bridge this gap by providing an easy-to-use digital platform for sharing information about surplus food and encouraging food donation.

---

## Our Solution

FoodBridge provides a centralized platform where users can:

- Explore available food donations
- View information about donated food
- Learn how the food donation process works
- Connect the concept of surplus food with people in need
- Promote awareness about food waste and responsible food sharing

---

## Features

-  **Responsive Homepage** – Clean and attractive landing page
-  **Food Donation Cards** – Displays different types of available food
-  **Responsive Design** – Works across different screen sizes
-  **Navigation Bar** – Easy navigation between sections
-  **Modern UI** – Simple and user-friendly interface
-  **Donation Information** – Provides useful information about food donation
- 🌍 **Social Impact Focus** – Encourages food sharing and reduction of food waste
