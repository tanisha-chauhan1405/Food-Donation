How FoodBridge works

Three steps between surplus food and someone who needs it.

Post surplus food

Donors list food type, quantity, and pickup window in under a minute.

NGOs get notified

Nearby verified NGOs see the post instantly and claim it before it expires.

Food gets picked up

Volunteers collect the food and it reaches people who need it, fast.
